# Example: email

The source and names below are fictional. The user confirmed that the expected date is Friday, October 9. Preserve that supplied date when rewriting.

## Before

> Hi Dana,
>
> I just wanted to circle back and touch base regarding the onboarding form. As mentioned on
> our call, we've been going back and forth on a few things, but basically the gist is that the
> form is now live (yay!) and new hires should be able to fill it out going forward. One thing
> that's important to note is that the manager approval step isn't hooked up yet, so for the
> time being HR will need to manually approve submissions. ETA on that is probably EOW. Let me
> know if you have any Qs!
>
> Thanks so much,
> Sam

## After

> Hi Dana,
>
> The onboarding form is live. New hires can fill it out starting today.
>
> The manager approval step is not connected yet. Human Resources needs to approve each
> submission by hand until it is. I expect to connect it by Friday, October 9.
>
> Reply here with any questions.

## What changed

- Removed the opening filler and the call reference.
- Led with the fact the reader needs first.
- Replaced "HR," "ETA," "EOW," and "Qs" with full words.
- Replaced "end of week" with an exact date.
- Split the long sentence so each sentence holds one idea.
- Kept the email format because the source was an email.
